# Career Progression and Applications

[Overview](README.md) · [Reference guide](reference_souces/Finance_Data_Analytics_Career_Guide_Canada_Updated.md)

## Target roles

Start with finance data analyst, finance BI analyst, financial reporting analyst, banking data analyst, or revenue analyst. Titles and requirements differ: inspect postings for the actual mix of SQL, Excel, BI, finance, education, location, and work authorization. The project fixture is fictional finance data, not evidence of banking or credit experience.

| Direction after foundation | Additional practice | Caveat |
|---|---|---|
| FP&A / financial analytics | Forecasting, drivers, scenario and three-statement modeling, partnering | CPA can be valuable in accounting-heavy roles; requirements vary. |
| Finance BI | Data modeling, reporting governance, SQL, DAX, definitions and stakeholder communication | PL-300 is optional. |
| Credit / risk | Credit data, probability, regression, model governance and independent validation | Do not treat a toy model as production-ready. FRM is optional and role-dependent. |
| Investment / portfolio | Financial statements, valuation, returns, benchmark and investment writing | CFA may be relevant; investment research can be competitive. |
| Quant research | Advanced probability, linear algebra, calculus, time series and serious modeling | Often requires substantial additional study or an advanced degree. |

## Milestones

- **Weeks 1–8:** Document one mini-project and review 20 target postings. Log title, location, date, required versus preferred skills, and recurring gaps.
- **Weeks 16–20:** If complete, publish Project A with reproducible data, SQL, dashboard and explanations; ask for feedback.
- **Weeks 21–32:** Build Project B and tailor applications as soon as the job requirements and your evidence match. Do not claim real finance outcomes from synthetic exercises.
- **Weeks 33–36:** Practise timed SQL and Excel exercises, dashboard explanation, a finance case, and behavioral stories. Continue improving based on feedback.

## Interview practice

1. Explain the grain and join cardinality; write a join, grouped KPI and window query, then diagnose duplicated totals.
2. Explain revenue, expense, profit, favorable variance, denominator selection, and why profit is not cash flow.
3. Present a Power BI measure and how filters change it; explain a missing-data or duplicate-row check.
4. Explain a pandas cleaning/aggregation workflow and reproduce one figure by hand.
5. Present a finding in two minutes: decision, evidence, uncertainty, recommendation, next data needed.

Use only true examples in STAR stories. A portfolio project's simulated scenario is not workplace impact.

## Canadian compensation research

The [reference guide](reference_souces/Finance_Data_Analytics_Career_Guide_Canada_Updated.md) gives directional 2026 estimates, explicitly not experience-specific observed medians. Recheck [Job Bank wages](https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/wagereport/occupation/12417) and current postings by city, occupation and date; compare base salary separately from bonus and benefits. Quant/investment compensation and qualifications vary widely, so do not generalize a single figure to all roles.
