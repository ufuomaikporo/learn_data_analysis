## Phase gate

- [ ] I can independently reproduce the deliverable and explain its grain, definitions and assumptions.
- [ ] I checked source counts, duplicates, missing data and relevant control totals.
- [ ] I can demonstrate a failure case and state what happens rather than hiding it.
- [ ] Outputs and instructions are reproducible; confidential data is absent.
- [ ] I can present the result, limitations and a sensible next question in two minutes.
- [ ] I logged gaps and schedule any remediation before the next phase.
