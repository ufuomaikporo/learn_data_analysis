# Curriculum and Schedule

[Overview](README.md) · [Weekly schedules](weekly-schedules/README.md) · [Labs](labs/README.md)

The [source guide](reference_souces/Finance_Data_Analytics_Career_Guide_Canada_Updated.md) calls for Excel, SQL, Power BI, statistics, Python, and two projects before choosing a specialization. The following preserves its 36-week sequence. One week means 8–10 hours; the cadence below allocates **9 hours**, including labs and projects, not in addition to them. Total: **324 planned hours**; the wider 8–10-hour range gives 288–360 hours.

| Days | Hours | Activity |
|---|---:|---|
| Day 1 | 1 | Selected lesson and notes |
| Day 2 | 1.5 | Guided exercise |
| Day 3 | 1.5 | Lesson and short practice |
| Day 4 | 3 | Lab or project build |
| Day 5 | 2 | Checks, documentation, explanation, and review |
| **Total** | **9** | |

| Phase | Weeks | Focus and measurable exit evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| [1 — Excel and finance](weekly-schedules/phase-01-excel-finance.md) | 1–4 | A budget-versus-actual workbook, reconciled formulas and one-page management summary. |

## Continuous tracks

- **Finance:** each week define the decision, KPI, reporting period, units, and assumptions; connect figures to revenue, cost, profit, and variance.
- **Communication:** write a concise finding, implication, limitation, and next question. Have someone review where possible.
- **Career:** inspect 20 relevant postings by Week 8; publish a first project around Weeks 16–20 when ready, and begin targeted applications once baseline requirements and evidence match roles.

## Assessment gates

At each phase end, independently reproduce the relevant lab, reconcile figures, explain a failure or limitation, and save evidence. If a gate fails, spend the next study block repairing it before starting the next phase; extend the calendar rather than compressing practice. Prior experience can justify skipping lessons, but not the exit evidence.

By Week 36, independently clean data, write intermediate SQL, model and explain Power BI measures, use pandas for analysis, and present two projects. This foundation does not qualify someone automatically for quant research, regulated model ownership, or a particular job.
