# Phase 4 — Statistics and Analytical Thinking, Weeks 17–20

[Schedule index](README.md) · [Phase 4 lab](../labs/phase-04-statistics.md)

**Time:** 4 × 9 = 36 hours. **Outcome:** A documented variance investigation with descriptive statistics, uncertainty and cautious interpretation. Primary: [W3Schools statistics](https://www.w3schools.com/statistics/). Supplements: [Udemy statistics](https://www.udemy.com/course/statistics-for-data-science-and-business-analysis/), [NIST statistics handbook](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/) and [OpenIntro Statistics](https://www.openintro.org/book/os/) for topics needing deeper treatment. Excel is sufficient; Python is introduced later.

### Weekly study map

| Week | Primary W3Schools topics | Supplement when needed |
|---|---|---|
| 17 | Mean, median, spread and distributions. | Udemy descriptive statistics; NIST for method details. |
| 18 | Descriptive statistics and relationships where covered. | Udemy or OpenIntro for correlation, confounding and interpretation. |
| 19 | Review distributions and sample versus population concepts. | OpenIntro or Udemy for sampling and confidence intervals; NIST for methods and assumptions. Use these when the W3Schools introduction is insufficient. |
| 20 | Review relationship and distribution concepts. | OpenIntro or Udemy for introductory regression, residuals and interpretation; NIST for method details. |

Select relevant sections, not whole courses or the whole textbook. Perform the textbook exercises and lab within the existing 9 hours per week.

## Weekly cadence and schedule

| Day | Duration | Focus |
|---|---:|---|
| Day 1 | 1 hour | Primary study |
| Day 2 | 1.5 hours | Lab practice |
| Day 3 | 1.5 hours | Study or lab practice |
| Day 4 | 3 hours | Lab and project work |
| Day 5 | 2 hours | Evidence and review |
| **Total** | **9 hours** | All project work is included |

Each scheduled Lab Exercise identifies the numbered step or steps to complete in that week's lab; follow them in ascending order. For three-step labs, Day 5 is a review of the final step. The final self-check question in each week's log names that week's concepts and asks how to apply them as a data analyst in the real world, with an example and explanation. The weekly Goal and Deliverables links open the full lab. Study-map supplements are optional unless noted as needed for a gap.
