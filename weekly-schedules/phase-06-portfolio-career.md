# Phase 6 — Portfolio and Career, Weeks 27–36

[Schedule index](README.md) · [Phase 6 lab](../labs/phase-06-portfolio-career.md) · [Project briefs](../projects/README.md) · [Career guide](../04-career-progression.md)

**Time:** 10 × 9 = 90 hours. **Outcome:** Two polished, independently reproducible projects and targeted job preparation. Use the existing artifacts; do not manufacture extra projects or real-world impact. Apply earlier if prepared.

For refresher study, return first to [W3Schools Excel](https://www.w3schools.com/excel/), [SQL](https://www.w3schools.com/sql/), [statistics](https://www.w3schools.com/statistics/), [Python](https://www.w3schools.com/python/) and [pandas](https://www.w3schools.com/python/pandas/). For Power BI, use [Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/powerplatform/power-bi/). The [course map](../02-courses-and-reading.md) lists supplementary Udemy courses and official references; revisit only the material needed to address a specific gap.

## Weekly cadence and schedule

| Day | Duration | Focus |
|---|---:|---|
| Day 1 | 1 hour | Primary study |
| Day 2 | 1.5 hours | Lab practice |
| Day 3 | 1.5 hours | Study or lab practice |
| Day 4 | 3 hours | Lab and project work |
| Day 5 | 2 hours | Evidence and review |
| **Total** | **9 hours** | All project work is included |

Each scheduled Lab Exercise identifies the numbered step or steps to complete in that week's lab; follow them in ascending order. For three-step labs, Day 5 is a review of the final step. The final self-check question in each week's log names that week's concepts and asks how to apply them as a data analyst in the real world, with an example and explanation. The weekly Goal and Deliverables links open the full lab. Study-map supplements are optional unless noted as needed for a gap.
